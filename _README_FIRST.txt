❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: Do I need Node.js?
A: Yes. Download from https://nodejs.org/ (takes 5 minutes)

Q: Is my data safe?
A: Yes! Everything is stored locally. No cloud, no tracking, no ads.

Q: Can I access it on my phone?
A: Yes, but it's easier on desktop. See DEPLOYMENT.md for remote access.

Q: What if I want to share it with classmates?
A: Use ngrok (see DEPLOYMENT.md). Everyone can access via a public URL.

Q: Will my data be saved?
A: Yes! Everything saves to studymate.db. Data persists after restart.

Q: What if something breaks?
A: Delete studymate.db and restart. Or use your exported backup.
